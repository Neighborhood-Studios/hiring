The role

This is a hands-on builder's role. You'll work directly with the studio team to turn ideas into testable products: writing code, shipping early versions & learning from real customers. Depending on your experience, you may own projects independently, help set technical direction, or grow into mentoring other engineers.

If you prefer long planning cycles and narrowly defined work, this probably isn't the right role. If you want to build quickly, learn across industries & put useful products in front of real customers — keep reading.

What you'll do
  • Ship practical web and mobile products — rough prototype to customer-usable in days or weeks.
  • Write full-stack code across React / Next.js, Node.js, TypeScript & the data stores each product needs.
  • Build the supporting pieces — auth, payments, APIs, background jobs — that get experiments live fast.
  • Use existing AI models and tools where they help; develop judgment for when simpler software wins.
  • Add testing, monitoring & safeguards that fit the stage — without overengineering an early experiment.
  • Make sensible build-versus-buy calls balancing speed, cost, quality & reliability.
  • Work closely with founders, designers & engineers; senior candidates may mentor and help us hire.

Many hyperlocal products live on a phone — React Native / Expo / iOS / Android experience helps but isn't required: store submissions, OTA updates, push, location, offline sync, real-time, maps & geofencing, camera, deep linking, fast cold starts.

How our engineers work
  • JavaScript and TypeScript first; Python when useful for AI or data work.
  • React interfaces (often Tailwind, sometimes Next.js); Node.js backend services.
  • Relational and document databases; vector stores when a product actually needs them.
  • Model APIs and AI tools to speed research, prototyping & focused customer-facing features.
  • Autonomy to determine the best solution — in our users' shoes, communicating clearly, staying curious.
What we're looking for

We're open across experience levels: strong software fundamentals, evidence you can build, learn & finish what you start, and a shipped web or mobile product — professional, startup, or a substantial personal project. We care more about what you can do and how quickly you learn than a title or years. Be ready to walk us through something you built: the problem, the choices, what went wrong & what you learned.

Beyond any particular technology: you can find a path forward. Unfamiliar domain, new framework, incomplete docs, ambiguous spec — you ask for help early, but you don't sit blocked. You investigate, make decisions & ship. Speed matters: think in hours, not days, for a first version. And you should care about design — notice when an experience feels off, then suggest and implement a thoughtful fix. Strong written and verbal English required.
