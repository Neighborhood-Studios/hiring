We're hiring an
idea runner

The studio wall is covered in stickies — 2,000 ideas a year. Your job is to grab the most promising ones and run: through research, landing pages, trusted-circle notes & the experiments that decide which two become companies.

The role

You own the front of the studio funnel. Every idea that earns conviction moves through our idea process — advocate → landing page → "why will this die?" note → mechanism or research → experiment — and you're the one keeping it moving. Fast, honest & documented.

This is not a strategy seat. It's a doing seat: calls in the wild, quick pages, tight write-ups & the discipline to kill an idea the moment the evidence says so.

What you'll do
  • Take a new idea from sticky note to a live landing page in Claude Design — same day when it's hot.
  • Run the trusted-circle Apple Note: gather the "main reason this business will die" answers and distill the core frictions.
  • Do the research that resolves a friction — fact-finding calls, market digging, getting into a fast follow's product and capturing every flow.
  • Help the advocate shape the mechanism: the Zestimate-style feature that makes the business work.
  • Stand up lean experiments with the studio team and keep their weekly signal reported on the KPI wall.
  • Keep the pipeline honest in Roam — what's alive, what's parked, what's dead and why.
Who thrives here

You're resourceful and unembarrassable — happy to cold-call a stranger, sign up for a competitor's service, or drive across Atlanta to watch how something really works. You write clearly and fast. You love ideas but love evidence more: you'd rather kill a favorite sticky than protect it. Comfort with AI tools (Claude Design especially) is a big plus; a portfolio of scrappy things you've validated — or killed — is even better.

"Burn your resume. Take risks. Immerse yourself in places where you are really going to learn. Get hands-on experience taking shots on goal around the best people building products."
— Mark Pincus, Life at the Speed of Play
